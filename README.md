# Sum Garden

A mental maths game for quant trading interview practice. Play quick games, earn coins, and grow pixel gardens across five biomes.

- **Sums**: 60-second rounds, typed or multiple choice, three difficulties (Hard includes fractions)
- **Word Sprout**: guess the 5-letter word in 6 tries
- **Letter Patch**: longest word from 9 letters in 30 seconds
- **Number Plot**: reach the target with 6 numbers in 60 seconds

There are about 85 real plant species to collect, each with a fact in the botany log, and wildlife that turns up depending on what you plant.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Open `index.html` to play. `LEARN.md` explains how the code works.

The word list in `words.js` comes from the MIT-licensed package `an-array-of-english-words`.
